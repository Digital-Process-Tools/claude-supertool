`_save_session`'s `os.chmod`/`os.fchmod` call in both `presets/bluesky/_atproto.py` and
`presets/watch/sources/bluesky-engagement/poller.py` sits inside a bare `except OSError: pass`.
If narrowing the mode fails for a reason other than "file just created cleanly" -- a read-only
filesystem, a permission-denied on a pre-existing file owned by another user, an EROFS mount --
the write proceeds silently and the session cache can end up sitting on disk at whatever mode it
had (which, before #2484's fix, could be the wide umask default). Nothing surfaces this to the
caller or a log; the function returns `None` either way.

Named out of scope for #2484 by that issue's own body ("chmod failure swallowed silently ... is
deliberately out of scope here"). Worth a look at raising a warning (or at minimum probing with
`os.chmod`/`stat` after the fact and refusing to proceed with a session it could not narrow)
rather than the current unconditional swallow -- unsure whether it should become its own issue or
get folded into a broader "declining instead of guessing" style fix for this module. Logged, not
decided.
