review_return.py's `--framed` classifier can misclassify a fully-stated review as `referred-not-stated` when the message ends with its own honest recap sentence.

Observed on issue #1868's self-review: the oss:auditor spawn's final message enumerated 3 findings in full (mechanism, verified reproduction command, severity, ranking row) under headed sections (`## Class B`, `## Class D/F/H`), then closed with a genuinely non-referential summary line: "Classes checked: 4 (A, B, C, D/F/H). Findings: 3 (2 under B, 1 under D/F/H). `could not check`: 0 full classes ... `unranked`/`could not rank`: 0."

Piped verbatim through `scripts/review_return.py --framed`, this returned `VERDICT: referred-not-stated`, quoting that trailing recap sentence as the back-reference evidence -- even though every finding was already fully stated above it, not merely gestured at.

Confirmed by re-running the classifier against the same message with only the trailing recap sentence removed: it returned `states-findings` cleanly, with the same 3 findings still present in the enumerable-block count. So the tool's back-reference heuristic appears to trigger on a closing "Findings: N" or "Classes checked: N" style summary sentence regardless of whether the findings above it were actually stated in full -- it cannot distinguish "as noted above" (a real gesture) from "here is a tally of what I just told you" (a legitimate closing line).

Cost: this forced a manual read of the full message to confirm the findings really were stated (they were), rather than trusting the classifier's computed verdict as the developer brief instructs ("compute it, do not read tone"). Reported here rather than silently overridden, per the brief's own instruction not to read tone over the classifier -- but also not silently accepted as `returned-nothing` in the report, since that would misrepresent a fully-delivered review as a lost one.

Not confirmed: whether this is specific to a "Classes checked: N... Findings: N..." phrasing, or any sentence near the end of a message that contains the token "Findings:" followed by a digit outside the leading header line. Left for whoever curates this to determine via `scripts/review_return.py`'s actual regex.
