- **`youtube_auth` and `youtube_comment`: the OAuth2 write half of the YouTube preset** ([#227](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/227)). `youtube_auth` runs a one-time PKCE loopback consent flow and caches a refresh token at `~/.config/youtube/oauth_token.json` (0600, created at that mode rather than chmod-ed after); `youtube_auth:status` reports AUTHORISED, NOT AUTHORISED or CANNOT TELL without touching the network. `youtube_comment` posts one top-level comment, then reads it back and reports `verified`, `MISMATCH` or `could-not-verify` as three separate states -- a 2xx from YouTube is not visibility, because programmatic comments are held or shadow-banned silently, and the output says so rather than leaving it to be assumed. Three guards refuse rather than warn, and the first two take different override tokens -- `|force` confirms the publish, `|force-dup` overrides the sentinel, and one does not grant the other, because `|force` is what an operator has to pass to publish at all and folding them together would have left the duplicate guard off on every invocation that published: `require_confirm`, the `~/.config/youtube/sent.jsonl` sentinel (one comment per video, 5 writes an hour, and a log that cannot be READ is `cannot-tell` and refuses too, because an unreadable log is not an empty one and this preset has no delete op), and the `[AI-generated]` marker unless `no_publish_disclosure` is set. Stdlib only, no vendored Google client. `youtube_reply`, `youtube_like` and `youtube_status_since` remain unwritten on purpose -- see `docs/presets/youtube.md`.
