- **A preset-supplied `builtin-ops.<op>.syntax` could make `help:OP` advertise payload keys `OP:@-` refuses.** `_build_at_file_registry` derived a built-in's `@payload` field names from any `builtin-ops` entry's `syntax` string, including the read-op family (`read`, `grep`, `around`, `grep_around`, `between`, `validate`) whose actual dispatch never consults that registry at all -- `_read_op_from_payload` looks up `_READ_OP_AT_FIELDS[op]` directly (`#625`). A manifest overriding `read`'s `syntax` made `help:read` print `Keys: where, howmuch` while `read:@-` still only accepted `path`, `offset`, `limit`, `grep`, `full` -- the refusal was honest but the documented route was wrong. `_build_at_file_registry` now leaves any op declared in `_READ_OP_AT_FIELDS` alone, regardless of what a config's `syntax` says.
