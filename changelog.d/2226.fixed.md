- **`git-resolve`'s receipt now says when a whole-file `ours`/`theirs` moved content outside the conflict it was asked to resolve** ([#2226](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2226)). `checkout --ours`/`--theirs` is git's own whole-file operation, not a bug: it replaces the whole file with the chosen side's committed version, not just the blocks `git-conflicts` numbered. A caller who had just read a one-block listing had no reason to expect a paragraph 300 lines away — auto-merged cleanly, carrying no markers — to be silently reverted too, and the old receipt (`markers: clean`, `Resolved: 1`) said nothing about it. A resolved file's receipt now carries a second line diffing the working tree immediately before and after the checkout, line by line against the conflict blocks' own spans, and naming anything outside every block by line range: `1 conflict block(s), 2 hunk(s) changed — 1 outside any conflict (lines 434-436)`. The `outside` clause is the signal, not the raw hunk-vs-block count (difflib's hunk count alone can differ from the block count even when nothing moved outside). Prints `outside-conflict check: not available (...)` instead of falling silent when the before/after snapshot could not be read — silence there would read exactly like the clean case. Silent (omitted) only for `both` (in-place marker rewrite, which structurally cannot touch anything outside a block). The classification subtracts each conflict block's own line coverage from every changed hunk rather than testing whole-hunk overlap, which a self-review caught missing content in a hunk that merely *touched* a block's boundary.
