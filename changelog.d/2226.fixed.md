- **`git-resolve`'s receipt now says when a whole-file `ours`/`theirs` moved content outside the conflict it was asked to resolve** ([#2226](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2226)). `checkout --ours`/`--theirs` is git's own whole-file operation, not a bug: it replaces the whole file with the chosen side's committed version, not just the blocks `git-conflicts` numbered. A caller who had just read a one-block listing had no reason to expect a paragraph 300 lines away — auto-merged cleanly, carrying no markers — to be silently reverted too, and the old receipt (`markers: clean`, `Resolved: 1`) said nothing about it. A resolved file's receipt now carries a second line comparing the working tree immediately before and after the checkout: `1 conflict block(s), 2 hunk(s) changed — 1 outside any conflict (lines 434-436)`. Silent when the two counts agree, when the before/after snapshot could not be read, or for `both` (in-place marker rewrite, which structurally cannot touch anything outside a block).
