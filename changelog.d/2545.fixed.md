- `_toml_delimiter_hint()` stayed silent on a `'''` embedded in prose that closes a value at the END of its own line (`"...closes like this: '''"`), rather than immediately before more text on the same line. The trailing garbage that early close produced landed on the NEXT line, past where the #1830 same-line check ever looked, so the payload raised a bare TOML parse error with no delimiter hint at all -- the shape the hint exists to explain, unexplained. `_toml_delimiter_early_close()` now walks past blank/comment lines to check whether what follows looks like a real next statement ([#2545](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2545)).
