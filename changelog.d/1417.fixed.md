- **#1417's refusal stated a cause that is false when the caller typed `.`** ([#1417](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1417)). `_parse_grep_args` produces `path == "."` from an empty PATH slot *and* from a literal one, and the refusal explained itself with "an empty slot defaults to it" in both — untrue for the second caller, in the one place they go to find out what they typed wrong. It now names the slot and both readings of it. The parser is deliberately not widened to carry the origin: its 6-tuple is unpacked at eleven sites, and the two inputs earn the same refusal for the same reason. `.supertool.json` and `docs/operations/search.md` carried the same half-claim and now say the same thing.
