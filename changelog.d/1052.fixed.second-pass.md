- **The new three-state zero printed a backwards line range** ([#1052](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1052)). Reading with a `grep=` filter at an offset past the end of the file produced `no lines matching 'x' in lines 1001-1000` — a span whose start is after its end. The counter naming the scanned range only advances inside the scan loop, and when the offset is past EOF that loop never runs. The count of unsearched lines was right all along; the range naming them was not, and a disclosure that reads as nonsense is not read at all. That case now says the offset is past the end and that nothing was searched.
