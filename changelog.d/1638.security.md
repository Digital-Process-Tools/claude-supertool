- ([#1638](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1638))
  `git-resolve` relayed three failed child streams into its receipt unmarked —
  `git checkout --ours/--theirs`'s stderr, the staging `git add`'s stderr, and
  the same `git add` on the block-selector path. Each reaches a
  `✗ PATH: REASON` row, and `_untrusted.split_lines` cuts on LF/CR/CRLF alone by
  design, so a U+2028 in that text puts chosen content at the start of a line
  where a `[result]` a consumer greps for sorts first. All three now go through
  `_untrusted.flat`, the treatment fifteen `presets/github/` sites got in #1622.
  The blast radius is narrower than that one's and worth stating: these are
  `git`'s own local streams on a merge the caller initiated, so reaching them
  means controlling a path name, a ref name, or a merge driver's output.
- The `cannot read: {e}` / `cannot write: {e}` reasons beside them are
  deliberately **not** flattened, and there is now a test saying why:
  `str(OSError)` reprs the filename, so a U+2028 in a conflicted path arrives
  already spelled as its escape — ASCII that cannot open a line. They were
  flattened in a first cut of this change and then un-flattened.
