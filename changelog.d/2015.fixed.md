- **A nested-pytest child spawned by `test_repo_target_673.py` / `test_gl_repo_target_676.py` no longer misreports a harness death as a product failure, and both now capture temp-path diagnostics around the spawn** ([#2015](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2015)). Five Windows CI occurrences saw the nested child die during collection on a temp-directory path -- twice under the legacy `C:\Documents and Settings` junction spelling and, on the fifth, under the canonical `C:\Users\...` spelling, which rules out canonicalisation as the root cause. Root cause is not established; this ships the instrumentation the issue itself asked for (`tests/_child_temp_diagnostics.py`'s `snapshot_temp_state()`, captured before and after the spawn) plus a real fix for a smaller, adjacent defect: `test_repo_target_673.py` had never received the #2067 harness-vs-product classifier that its GitLab twin already had, so an occurrence there would have read as "the child pytest ran and disagreed" -- it did not. Both files now share `tests/_nested_pytest_verdict.py`.
