- **A nested-pytest child spawned by `test_repo_target_673.py` / `test_gl_repo_target_676.py` no longer misreports a harness death as a product failure, both now capture temp-path diagnostics around the spawn, and the child now gets a private `TMP`/`TEMP`/`TMPDIR` of its own** ([#2015](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2015)). Six Windows CI occurrences saw the nested child die during collection on a temp-directory path -- twice under the legacy `C:\Documents and Settings` junction spelling, on the fifth under the canonical `C:\Users\...` spelling (which rules out canonicalisation as the root cause), and on the sixth inside `go-build3225713325`, a directory this suite does not own. Root cause is not established; what ties all six together is a listing-then-stat race on a temp directory shared with unrelated processes, not which directory happened to fail. This ships the instrumentation the issue itself asked for (`tests/_child_temp_diagnostics.py`'s `snapshot_temp_state()`, captured before and after the spawn), a real fix for a smaller, adjacent defect (`test_repo_target_673.py` had never received the #2067 harness-vs-product classifier that its GitLab twin already had, now shared via `tests/_nested_pytest_verdict.py`), and a mitigation of unknown sufficiency: `tests/_isolated_child_tmp.py`'s `child_env_with_private_tmp` gives the spawned child a fresh, empty, private temp root so nothing else on the runner can race it there.
