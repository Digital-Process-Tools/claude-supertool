- **`gh-prs`'s `syntax` was 243 characters against a p90 of 66, because a sentence had been appended to a parameter list** ([#1590](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1590)). Every consumer prints the field whole — the `ops` roster, `registry`, `help:gh-prs`, and the synonym suggestion [#1524](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1524) added — so the invocation a caller needed began around character 255 of the rendered line. Measured across every shipped preset: 86 ops declare a `syntax`, and exactly 1 carried a double-dash clause or an issue number. The #1524 reviewer refused to teach the render to split on that separator, on the grounds that a convention inferred from one field is not a convention, and was right; the defect was the field.

  **The guarantee did not have to move, because it was never on the prose.** #1405 pinned the substring `merged-since=TAG,state=merged` into `syntax` so a deleted op's replacement could not go missing — and that substring is an *invocation*, not a sentence. It stays, as the second of two ` | `-separated forms, which is the shape `gh-check`, `bluesky_list`, `devto_list` and `hashnode_list` already use for an alternate call. What left is the narration around it, which `description` already carried word for word and where #1405's own test also pins it. Both existing pinning tests pass unchanged.

  The convention is now written down in `docs/contributing.md` and enforced across the whole corpus rather than on the one field: no `syntax` carries an issue reference, none carries a double-dash clause, none exceeds 200 characters (the longest pure grammar in the tree is `gl-mrs` at 165), and every ` | `-separated form begins with its own op's name.
