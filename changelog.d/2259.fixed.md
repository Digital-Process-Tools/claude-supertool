- **The Windows Defender exclusion step in `tests.yml` no longer fails the whole `pytest (windows-latest, …)` leg when `Add-MpPreference` itself fails** ([#2259](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2259)). The step is a pure disk-cost optimisation and touches nothing correctness-bearing, but had no failure tolerance — on runner infrastructure where the Defender service is unavailable or disabled, `Add-MpPreference` returned `0x800106ba` and took the entire job down before a single test ran, rendering a red that looked exactly like a real regression. The step now carries `continue-on-error: true`, with a comment recording why, so a Defender hiccup on the runner degrades to a slower green instead of a meaningless red.
