- `grep` and `around` now refuse any top-level alternation branch that matches every line, not just an empty one (#1314). The #1120 guard tested for `b == ""`, so `grep:^\|def op_:PATH` — the bash-BRE rewrite turning `\|` into a top-level `|` with `^` to its left — sailed through and reported 5 of "1000+ matches" for a pattern matching every line of the file. A branch is saturating when it matches the empty string, which is what `^`, `$`, `.*` and `z*` have in common; the refusal now names the offending branch. `^$|alpha`, `(^|,)alpha`, `colo(u|)r` and `[|]b` are all still searches and still run.
