- A failing `git add` under `git-commit` used to answer every caller with `Paths are separated by ':::' — not by commas, not by spaces`, whatever route they were on and whatever the pathspec held. On the `@payload` route that is the *other* route's convention quoted at someone who followed this one, and two agents in one afternoon re-read the syntax they had got right; the payload refusal now names the shape this route wants — `paths` is a TOML array, one entry per path — and offers the split of a `:::`- or `,`-joined entry as a question. Where no separator is in play at all, a single path spelled wrong, the note is now withheld rather than reworded: git already named the fault and there is no one-line remedy to add, and a fresh wrong remedy reads as a freshly checked one (#1489).
