- **`gl-job:trace` and `gl-pipeline:traces` could spend more subprocess budget per call than their own declared op timeout allowed for, discarding every trace already fetched at the kill** (#2105). `_fetch_trace_and_meta` spends up to 10s (metadata) + 20s (trace) = 30s per job id, serially — `gl-job` declared 30s while accepting comma-separated multi-id `:trace` calls (#2095), and `gl-pipeline` declared 15s for `:traces`, which fans out over every failed job in the pipeline. `write_traces` now caps how many ids one call actually fetches (`GL_JOB_TRACE_MAX_IDS`, default 6 — already the threshold the filename-safety logic used), and names any id beyond the cap as not-fetched rather than attempting it and risking the kill; `gl-job`'s and `gl-pipeline`'s declared timeouts are raised (30s to 210s, 15s to 220s) to comfortably cover that many ids serially with margin.
