- New op `payload-lint:@FILE` parses an @file/@- payload and reports its
  shape -- fields, provenance (triple-single-quoted literal /
  triple-double-quoted basic / single-line), length, line count,
  backslash/CR/trailing-whitespace flags -- and runs nothing. For an ops
  array: op count, kinds, and resolved target paths. For an edit/replace-
  shaped payload: whether `old` matches the target right now, and how many
  times, as a snapshot, without writing. Previously the only way to see what
  a malformed triple-quoted block actually parsed to was to apply the
  payload and read the target file back -- and for `edit`, a malformed
  payload does not even refuse: it matches, writes, and reports `edited`
  (#1032).
