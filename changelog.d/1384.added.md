- The GitLab ops declare what raw `glab` invocation they supersede, so the shipped `PreToolUse` guard covers `glab` as well as `gh` ([#1384](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1384)). `glab mr view`, `glab mr list`, `glab issue view`, `glab issue create`, `glab ci trace` and `glab ci get --pipeline-id` are refused in favour of the op that answers them, with `--comments` selecting the `:full` view. `gl-api` and `gl-runners` declare nothing: `glab api` becomes a write under `-X`/`-F`/`--input` and supertool has no route for a GitLab write, so blocking it would dead-end the command `gl-api` itself points writers at.
