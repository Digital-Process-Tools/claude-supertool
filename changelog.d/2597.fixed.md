- The token-file mode-bit probe (`_publish_safety._mode_bits_are_enforced`) is now created next to the credential it is judging rather than in `TMPDIR` -- a probe on a different mount than the credential (exFAT/FAT/SMB, a container `/tmp`) could take the soft `WARNING` arm for a genuinely group/world-readable credential that lived on an enforcing filesystem. A credential directory that is missing or not writable falls back to the old behaviour, reported on stderr rather than silently (#2597).
