The two arms #1551 added (`_at_file_to_parts`, and the batch-wrapper unknown-key arm) plus three pre-existing siblings — the batch sub-op unknown-field and no-declared-field-order arms, and `_read_op_from_payload`'s unknown-field arm. The issue named four; the sweep found five, and the fifth is reachable with any batch sub-op that has no declared payload field order. An ordinary key is printable and renders byte-identically, non-ASCII included. `lower_payload` lowercased the key on four of the five, which bounded the forge to lowercase prose and did not close it — every op's own body text is lowercase prose, and the batch-wrapper arm sorted the raw keys, so an uppercase `SYSTEM:` line was reachable there (#1583).
