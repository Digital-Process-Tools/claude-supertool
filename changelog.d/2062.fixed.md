- **`classify` and `slack_publish` now pin their stdout to UTF-8 before printing, closing a Windows-console crash on the entry point's own success path** ([#2062](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2062)). Neither `main()` called `use_utf8_stdout()`, unlike 44 other preset entry points -- on `classify`, a `suspect` report can embed a fence-forgery glyph (U+27E8) straight from the matched text, and the repo's own AST census (`tests/test_encoding_seam.py`) cannot see the gap because that print is computed (`print(run(text))`) rather than a non-ASCII string literal, which is the shape the census matches. Reproduced directly against the real entry point under a non-UTF-8 console codepage: the process died reporting a verdict it had already reached, so the work landed and the receipt said it crashed (#415's shape). Whether the census itself should be widened to catch a computed print is a separate, unfiled design question.
