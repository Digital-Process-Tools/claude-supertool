- **Three tests were reaching the live internet, and all three passed on whatever came back** ([#1312](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1312)). `test_hashnode.py::test_comment_main_aborts_on_dupe` stubbed the op's `gql` and left `gql_safe` — which is the one the pre-flight actually calls — so every leg of every PR opened a real HTTPS connection to `gql.hashnode.com`. It went red on PR #1302, a diff of one test file and one markdown table, when Hashnode began answering 301: the redirect guard refused the off-origin hop and said no credentials were sent, correctly, in a message with no `ABORT` in it. The product was right and the harness rendered it as a product defect.

  Two more in `test_security_error_echo_691.py` had the same shape one layer along. They patched `MODULE.urllib.request.urlopen`, but every preset routes through `_http.urlopen`, which calls `_http._OPEN` — bound to the opener at import and never looked up on `urllib.request` again. The stub replaced a name nothing calls, so `bsky.social` and `dev.to` were contacted for real. The bluesky one is the sharp case: it exists to prove that a password echoed back by a remote is redacted, and the echo it injects had never once been delivered.

  All three are stubbed now, at the seam the product reads, and each assertion was tightened so it can only be satisfied by the injected body — `main()` has three `ABORT` arms, and the hashnode test had spent its life green on the wrong one.

  `tests/_netblock.py` is the guard: `block_outbound(monkeypatch)` refuses any non-loopback `connect` or `getaddrinfo` and names the host and what to stub, so a missing stub fails at the socket instead of being answered by the internet. Armed in both touched modules. Loopback and `AF_UNIX` stay open — `test_http_bounds.py` and the `claude-channel` suites bind real servers and are hermetic.

  The count is measured, not grepped: the whole suite was run under a socket observer that recorded every outbound attempt (`10186 passed`, 285s). Three tests, three hosts, and nothing else in 581 test files.
