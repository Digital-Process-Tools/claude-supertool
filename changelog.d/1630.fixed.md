- **The #1232 symlink-gating register no longer flags a bare helper `UNGATED` just because the gate lives on every one of its callers instead of on the helper itself** ([#1630](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1630)). `_Visitor._mechanism` only ever inspected the module's `pytestmark` and the decorators on the call site's own enclosing `def`, never a caller -- so a bare helper with no decorator of its own read `UNGATED` even when every one of its callers was correctly platform-gated. Hit live on PR #1620 (2026-08-13): `test_guard_envelope_serialisation_1613.py::_toolbox` was referenced only from `_run`, whose six callers all carried `@_POSIX_ONLY`, and 13 of 23 CI legs went red on a fixture that was already correct. Adds a third recognised mechanism, `C`, via a new `@gated_by_caller` decorator in `tests/_symlink.py`: applied to the helper's own `def` line, it lets the visitor's existing decorator walk see the convention directly, with no interprocedural call-graph analysis needed -- the cheaper of the two remedies the issue laid out, and the one its author preferred.
