- **`git-status:brief`** ([#1028](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1028)). Drops the local-branch inventory and the last-5-commits log, so the working tree and the MR/PR block — what the op is usually called for — sit near the top instead of below a dozen lines about other branches. That verbosity is what creates the incentive to pipe the op through `tail`, which is a documented mistake here because these ops put the meaning first; a flag removes the incentive rather than the symptom.

  The default is deliberately untouched: somebody depends on the branch list, and changing what a bare `git-status` prints is a behaviour change for every existing caller. Brevity drops only sections that were **answered** — a working tree or stash list that could not be read still renders its `UNKNOWN` marker, and the `git-status INCOMPLETE` footer still appears, because dropping those would be the absence-as-answer defect wearing a flag.
