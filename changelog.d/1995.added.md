- **`gh-pr-create` and `gh-pr-edit` now have an end-to-end ambient-repo-target test through their own `main()`, closing the coverage asymmetry #1990/#1993 left behind** ([#1995](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1995)). All four payload-mode write ops route through `resolve_or_conflict()` -> `explicit_target()`, and that chokepoint already has its own unit test -- but only `gh-issue-create` and `gl-issue-create` had a test proving an inherited `SUPERTOOL_REPO` with no `repo:` op in the call is ignored all the way through `main()`. A later change bypassing `explicit_target()` in `pr_create.py` or `pr_edit.py` alone would have left every existing guard green. `tests/test_gh_pr_create_main_950.py` and `tests/test_gh_pr_edit_1739.py` each gain one such case, reusing the file's own existing harness (`_Harness`/`_install`/`_payload` and `_install_edit_harness`/`_pr_payload` respectively) rather than extracting a shared fixture -- the two harnesses mock different shapes (`gh` vs `gh_json`, base/head/check-tally resolution vs a PATCH body comparison) closely enough to their own op that unifying them now would cost more than the two bespoke additions did. Test-only; no product behaviour changed.
