- **A malformed `changelog.d/` fragment is now refused at write time, by the release script's own guard** ([#1132](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1132)). A fragment is written by an ordinary `paste` or `edit`, so nothing in the write path had ever looked at its content: the receipt said `git-status : ok`, and the first thing to disagree was the full CI matrix. PR #1115 went red on 14 of 20 legs over one missing `- `, and the same shape had bitten three authors in one evening.

  The new `changelog-fragment` validator **states no rules of its own.** It imports the project's `.github/scripts/assemble_changelog.py` and calls the three checks `collect()` calls — `parse_fragment_name`, the empty-body test, `scan_fragment_body` — publishing their messages verbatim. Those messages are already precise, and the failure mode of writing a second, thinner one is that the two drift: a green local receipt followed by a red matrix is the reported complaint inverted, and worse than it.

  Scope is a path glob in `.supertool.json` and the rules are the project's own script, found by walking up from the file. A project with no such script gets `skipped`, never `ok` — supertool runs against repos that have never heard of `changelog.d/`. So does a fragment `markdown-it-py` cannot be parsed for, which is the assembler's own `CannotValidate` rather than a guess made here.
