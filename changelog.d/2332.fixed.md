- **`git-worktrees`' `since=` declaration now rejects non-finite and future values instead of misreporting age** ([#2332](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2332)). `_parse_since` validated its input with `float()` plus a single `seconds_ago < 0` check, which never fires for `inf`, `nan` or an overflowing literal like `1e400` (none of which raise `ValueError`), and the `@<epoch>` form had no bound against `now` at all. The ordinary trigger is a caller typing a millisecond epoch where the documented contract wants seconds — the resulting huge future epoch produced a negative age that rendered as "0s ago", a bad declaration read back as maximally fresh rather than reported as bad input. `since=` now refuses `inf`/`nan`/an overflowing value and any `@<epoch>` after `now`, each as its own reported declaration error.
