- **A pytest session in which every core-timeout-gated call declined now exits non-zero instead of reading as a pass** ([#1523](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1523)). [#1501](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1501) gave 42 unit-test call sites the right to decline when the core's own spawn wall fires, and nothing put a floor under that right: a uniformly loaded runner mutes the whole population at once, pytest prints `0 failed`, and the suite has said nothing at all about the validator core. `tests/_core_timeout_census.py` counts the gated calls themselves — not skipped reports, whose denominator moves with the platform — and prints `D of C gated calls declined` in the terminal summary whether the count is zero or not.

  Three states, not two: `NOT CHECKED` when no gated call ran in the selection (which is not a pass), the ratio when some declined, and a finding when *all* of them did. The bar is categorical rather than a percentage because the measured baseline is zero — `0 of 61` on a full macOS run — so any percentage would be a number nobody measured, and a false red on a slow Windows leg is the exact publication #1501 exists to prevent.

- **A `SUPERTOOL_WATCH_NAME` exported in a maintainer's shell can no longer decide what the test suite measures** ([#1523](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1523)). `tests/conftest.py` has cleared the three watch variables since the [#1502](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1502) fix, and nothing asserted that it does. CI exports none of them, so deleting those three lines was invisible to every leg while costing a maintainer five reds about their shell rather than their diff — a guard that was itself unguarded. Now pinned by a probe that resolves the socket path against `naming.resolve({})` rather than a `/tmp` literal, so the assertion means the same thing on Windows.
