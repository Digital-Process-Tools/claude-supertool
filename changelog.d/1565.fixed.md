- The `supertool-no-cut` jit rule spanned a command separator, so a *different* command's pipe refused the op before it: the wildcard between the op string and the bar excluded only a newline, and walked straight through `;` and `&&`. A compound call whose `tail -2` belonged to the `pytest` after the `&&` was blocked, with a refusal naming a cut that was not happening — three wrong blocks on 2026-08-13, on top of the four in one evening #1433 records. The span now stops at `;` and at `&&` (#1565).
- `|` and a bare `&` deliberately stay inside that span, against the issue's own proposal to exclude all three separators. A genuine cut of an op's output routinely has both: the cut need not be the first stage (`gh-job:N:raw` into `grep` into `awk` is real, and was already pinned), and `2>&1` before the bar is the commonest spelling of one in this repo's transcripts. Only `&` followed by another `&` or by whitespace is a separator, so the span reads `([^;&\n]|&[^&[:space:]])*` rather than a flat negated class. Both directions are pinned through awk itself, and the fix was driven end-to-end through the real hook: eleven of eleven, four false blocks gone with no real cut lost (#1565).
