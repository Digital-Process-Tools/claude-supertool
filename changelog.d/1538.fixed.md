This repository named its own watch channel `oss-supertool` in `.supertool.json` and never told `.mcp.json`, so its producers bound `/tmp/supertool-watch-oss-supertool.sock` while the consumer bound the default — every event emitted here was lost at the source. `channel:health` reported it correctly; nothing pinned the two files against each other, which is what let a two-file change ship as a one-file change (#1538).
