- Every op in every shipped preset is now either mapped to the raw command it supersedes or recorded as a deliberate absence with its reason ([#1384](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1384)). `replaces` reached 22 of 87 ops over four PRs and nothing said which of the rest were decided and which were never looked at; the two render identically. `tests/test_replaces_census_1384.py` partitions the population, asserts it total in both directions, and goes red on an op that belongs to neither.
- Builtin ops (`read`, `grep`, `glob`, `tree`, `wc`) stay outside the guard, and the reasoning is written down rather than left implicit: the matcher scores every pipeline segment, so `cat` and `grep` mappings would fire on a stage of nearly every composed command; the only escape is `raw_command_guard: false`, which is repo-global and would take the forge mappings with it; and the payoff would be round-trips rather than the wrong answers every existing mapping prevents.
