The docstring and the #1130 register now scope the claim correctly, and `test_split_lines_cr_arm_is_dead_for_every_git_ops_caller` pins the narrower, accurate claim against `_git_verbatim`'s own two callers, which both already avoid `split_lines` for exactly that reason. Separately, `_looks_like_success` word-anchored its demotion keywords the same way #1669 word-anchored `_ERROR_WORD_RE`: `"fatal" in low` matched inside `"nonfatal"`, reading a genuine success line as not-success.
