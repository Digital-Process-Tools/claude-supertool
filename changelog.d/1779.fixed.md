- **`radar`'s GitHub PR board footer now names the discovery feed's own state, and a dead, capped, blind or coverage-unknown feed costs the tier's health verdict** ([#1779](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1779)). The footer used to print the fixed sentence `discovery: radar ticks only` on every board, whether or not that was still true — and a consumer who forgot to re-run `radar` after opening a PR, or after a feed died, had nothing telling them membership had gone stale between heals. #1780's `github-pr-feed` is what makes that sentence false most of the time now; this issue is the footer, `_feed_warnings` and the tier's `healthy` verdict actually saying so — `discovery: feed ok` / `feed DOWN` / `feed DOWN (respawn capped)` / `feed coverage UNKNOWN (#673)` — instead of a claim of coverage the tier could not back up. Closed together with #1780: a working feed is what removes the cost this issue measured, and a feed that goes quiet is the one case #1780 alone cannot cover, which is exactly what this footer and health-verdict change now surfaces.
