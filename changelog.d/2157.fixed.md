- **`git-resolve both`'s setext-heading duplicate guard read only the single line immediately above a `===`/`---` underline as the heading's title.** CommonMark allows a setext title to span multiple consecutive lines, so a heading written that way was recognised under the wrong (truncated) name and its real duplicate across a union went unseen -- staged clean over a document that would reparent everything under the first copy once merged. `_heading_paths` (`presets/git/resolve.py`) now walks back over the whole run of consecutive, unconsumed title lines above the underline instead of just the last one. The walk-back stops at a blank line, a heading, a fence delimiter, a list item or blockquote marker, or another thematic break, same as it always refused to fold those into a title -- and the existing single-`-` list-item ambiguity and thematic-break guards are re-verified rather than assumed still correct, since a wider lookback gives both more text to misread.
