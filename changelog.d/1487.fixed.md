- **`_validator_render_row`'s `code` field is now flattened like every other adapter-supplied field, matching its own docstring** ([#1487](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1487)). The docstring stated "every field the adapter supplies goes through `_flat_cell`"; `msg`, `tool`, `resolved_to` and `source_context` did, and `code` -- rendered raw in five call sites across `_validator_render_row`, `_validator_render_diff` and the formatter renderer -- did not. Every shipped adapter emits a fixed literal there today, so nothing on disk rendered wrong, but an adapter author who trusted the docstring and put remote text in `code` would have been correct to. All five sites now route it through `_flat_cell`.

  See [#1040](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1040) for this issue's other finding, the `repo:` leading-dash character-class gap, fixed together with #1040 in the same PR.
