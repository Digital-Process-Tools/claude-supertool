- **Fixed `gh-pr-create` publishing a body carrying literal backslash-quote sequences without a word** ([#1967](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1967)). A JSON payload's author who doubles the escaping of a quote inside `body` produces a decoded string with a real backslash immediately in front of a real quote — `paste`/`edit` already refuse exactly this shape (#1096), but `gh-pr-create` treated `body` as opaque and published it unread. It now scans the decoded body before publishing and refuses, naming every occurrence by line and column; `literal_backslashes = true` at the top level of the payload publishes the body unchanged when the run is meant as written (a shell snippet, a regex, a Windows path).
