- **The grep catastrophic-backtracking guard now catches a nested unbounded group inside an outer group that is not itself quantified** ([#2535](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2535)). `_has_outer_wrapped_unbounded_group` (#1311) resolved a `(...)` group by scanning to its matching close-paren and then jumped straight past the whole group (`i = j`), so a nested group buried inside it -- e.g. `(x(a+)+y)`, where the outer group has no trailing `+`/`*` of its own but the inner `(a+)` does -- was never independently checked for its own trailing quantifier. `(x(a+)+y)` is confirmed catastrophic: elapsed against 26 `a`s doubled per character. The scan now advances one character at a time instead of skipping past a resolved group, so every `(` in the pattern, at any nesting depth, gets its own check. Found by the gate-3 release-audit round 2 for the v0.60.0..HEAD delta. `tests/test_grep_backtracking_guard_nested_unquantified_outer_2535.py` is the regression coverage.
