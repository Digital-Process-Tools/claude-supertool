`read:PATH:A:B` discloses the OFFSET:LIMIT reading on the shape that was actually filed. The note added in #382 was gated on the window overrunning EOF, and the reported call (`read:_supertool.py:5370:5460` on a 19571-line file) does not overrun — so the warning stayed silent on the exact call it was written for. It now also fires when the second number is less than twice the first, the tell that separates an END line (which lands near its START) from an independent LIMIT; `read:PATH:10:20` and other ordinary skip-then-read calls stay quiet. The note also reports the window that was *asked for* rather than a line count it claims to have read: the byte cap can truncate the window, and on the filed call it did, so the old wording stated a number the call never produced.
