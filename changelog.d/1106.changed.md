- **`no error pattern matched` meant two opposite things, so the refusal header moved** ([#1106](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1106)). The phrase appeared both inside a *successful* classification — `gap_marker`'s aside about the lines between two anchors — and as the header of a *refusal to classify at all*. `gh-job` and `gl-job` now refuse with `## FAILED — supertool could not classify this job`. The body, the patterns-tried line, the tail and the three-state disclosure are unchanged.

    **It had already cost something.** Two of [#1099](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1099)'s tests asserted `"no error pattern matched" not in out` as shorthand for "the refusal header is absent", went red after rebasing onto [#1091](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1091), and were red against entirely correct code — the phrase had simply started appearing in healthy output. The next readers are worse placed: a grep over saved output, a watch rule, an agent scanning a render to decide whether a red leg was classified. All of them see a string rather than a section, the elision notice can appear many times in one render, and the refusal appears once at the top.

    **The marker's wording won, deliberately.** `the log itself is intact` is the only clause separating *this op cut lines* from *the log was truncated*, and [#1014](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1014) was filed this cycle on precisely that misread. Shortening it to break the collision would have reintroduced the confusion it exists to prevent.

    **Not one shared constant — a test.** A preset runs with `presets/` on `sys.path` and cannot import the core, and the two job presets are private twins by design; `gap_marker` is duplicated for that reason and pinned by `tests/test_gl_job_gap_marker_twins_1066.py`. `tests/test_job_refusal_header_collision_1106.py` does the same job for the header, and pins the stronger property: the refusal and any gap marker may share no run of four consecutive words. So the next collision is a red build rather than a coincidence two people have to notice, and it survives the next rewording of either string.
