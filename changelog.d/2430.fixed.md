- **`slack_authorization` no longer prints a raw config-file key at column 0** ([#2430](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2430)). `presets/slack/auth.py` read `channel_id` -- a key straight out of `~/.config/supertool/slack_authorization.json` -- and printed it unflattened at the start of a line in both `_list_all()` (the bare listing) and `_one()` (the per-channel header). A key carrying an embedded newline could render as two physical lines, the second indistinguishable from a genuine channel row -- the exact rendering shape `_supertool.py` already flattens for an `"ops"`/`"aliases"` config key via `_flat_field(..., disclose_newline=True)`. Both sites now route `channel_id` through the preset-side equivalent, `_untrusted.flat(channel_id, disclose_newline=True)`, with a new regression test pinning that a newline in the key cannot forge an extra row, paired with a positive control confirming two genuinely distinct channels still render as two separate rows.
