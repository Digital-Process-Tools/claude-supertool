- **`worktree:teardown`'s `copy` entries are no longer deleted unconditionally — a `copy` entry the worktree has since modified is left in place unless `worktree:teardown[:PATH]:force` is given** ([#2429](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2429)). `copy` is declared in `.supertool.json` for "anything machine-specific or the worktree might mutate" — exactly the kind of entry whose content is expected to diverge from what `setup` wrote — but `_remove_copy` had no staleness check comparable to `link`'s own `_same_target`, so a legitimately-edited `copy` entry was destroyed the same way an untouched one was. `setup` now records a `(size, mtime_ns)`-based fingerprint per `copy` entry (recursively for a directory) right after copying it; `teardown` recomputes the same fingerprint before deleting and refuses when it differs, is missing (an older manifest predating this check), or could not be recomputed — three states, not two, erring toward refusing when the tool cannot tell rather than deleting. An unmodified `copy` entry is unaffected and is still torn down cleanly with no `:force` needed.
