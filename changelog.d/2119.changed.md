- **`docs/input-forms.md` now states, in the op's own contract, that a doubled-backslash refusal is a whole-payload unit by construction, not an unexamined default** ([#2119](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2119)). The scan runs at parse time, over the raw source, before any op exists to attach a partial result to, so there is no unit smaller than "the payload" to accept or refuse -- the same argument that already scopes `literal_backslashes` to the top level rather than an `[[ops]]` table. No mechanism changed: the per-occurrence location (#1808, #1814) and the per-field exemption (#1839) already shrink what a re-send has to fix, and this closes the remaining ask -- a sentence saying whether whole-payload is the only coherent unit, so a caller does not have to rediscover that by re-reading the source.
