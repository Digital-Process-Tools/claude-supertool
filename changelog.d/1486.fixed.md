- **A file under validation could mint itself an extra finding, and `count` is baseline arithmetic** ([#1486](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1486)). Every validator adapter parsed its tool's stdout with `str.splitlines()`, which breaks on U+2028, U+2029, U+0085, VT and FF as well as on LF/CR/CRLF. No analyser frames its output that way, and most echo the source text they are complaining about verbatim into the diagnostic — so one of those five characters inside a string literal ended the adapter's idea of a line mid-diagnostic, the fragment re-matched the adapter's own regex, and it was published as a second record. Reproduced on `go-vet`: one diagnostic from `go vet`, `count: 2` on the receipt.

  Not cosmetic. `count` is the number `_validator_regressed` subtracts, so the file being checked partly chose its own baseline, and on a `rollback_on_fail` validator that baseline reverts edits.

  `validators/common/linebreaks.py` is the local `split_lines()` — `presets/_untrusted.split_lines` ([#1081](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1081)) restated because `validators/` cannot import from `presets/`, pinned equal to the core's own definition by a test.

  All 31 call sites were partitioned rather than swept. **Nineteen fixed**, in `go-vet`, `tsc-check`, `markdownlint`, `hadolint`, `cargo-check`, `xmllint`, `ruby-check`, `bash-check`, `gofmt-check`, `phpmd`, `phpstan`, `phplint`, `lsp-diag`, `terraform-check`, `jit-index` and `common/source_context.py`. **Twelve refused with a reason**: four JSON-RPC frame readers over `bytes`, where a mis-split fragment fails `json.loads` and can withhold a response but never mint one; four `traceback.format_exc()` reads of our own text; two message flatteners (`terraform-check.plain()`, `pyright`) where splitting on *more* separators is what neutralises them; and two quotes of `awk`'s own output. A test walks the tree and refuses any new call site that is neither fixed nor on that list with its reason.

  Two of the nineteen are not extra records but the same seam elsewhere. `lsp-diag` looked for a line starting `diag:` to detect an infrastructure failure, so a diagnostic's own text could turn a real verdict into a `skipped`. `common/source_context.py` numbered the *source file* by `str.splitlines()` while every tool numbers by LF, so a separator anywhere above a finding printed the wrong source line under a correct line number, for all thirty adapters at once.
