- The stock refusal patterns in `validators/common/refusal.py` now carry their provenance. `"--paths allowlist"` and what `refusal.outside_roots()` emits (`path outside <VAR> allowlist`) were read as a drifted pair; they are two mechanisms that never meet — the patterns classify the *analyser's* own message, and `outside_roots()` is a local short-circuit whose string goes straight to `skipped()` — and neither side was wrong. `"--paths allowlist"` is live: it is a substring of the daemon's measured emission `analyse: path is outside the configured --paths allowlist.`. Each pattern is now paired in the suite with the emission it was written for, or named explicitly as a spelling variant nobody has seen fire, so a pattern that matches nothing stops being indistinguishable from a refusal that never happened (#1548).
