- **The four warm MCP validator adapters (`phpstan-mcp`, `phpunit-mcp`, `phpmd-mcp`, `rector-mcp`) now retry once against a freshly-respawned daemon before reporting `NOT CHECKED` on a desynchronised pipe** ([#2449](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2449)). The shared daemon serves one client at a time; a client that disconnects mid-exchange can leave bytes in the socket buffer that the *next* client inherits, sees a JSON-RPC response addressed to somebody else's id, and — until now — timed out and reported `NOT CHECKED` with no retry, regardless of whether the write that triggered the check had already been accepted. `validators/common/ndjson_scan.receive_until` now raises a distinct `DesyncDetected` when a well-formed response to a *foreign* id (neither the awaited one nor the exchange's own `initialize` id) is seen, and each adapter's `ndjson_call` retries the whole exchange exactly once against a daemon force-respawned via the new `presets/mcp/_spawn.force_respawn`. Plain silence, or noise that never parses as a JSON-RPC response at all, is unaffected and still reports `NOT CHECKED` unretried -- respawning on that signal would double the wait for a daemon or analysis that is genuinely slow or gone, for no chance of recovery.
