- **`unwatch` reaching zero of this channel's pollers now says when the slot is covered by another channel instead of staying silent** ([#1893](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1893)). `cmd_unwatch` and `reap_duplicate_pollers` are correct to act only on this channel — [#1514](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1514) settled that, and killing another channel's poller is a wrong kill. What it did not settle was whether their existence may be *stated*, and `cmd_unwatch`'s own "nothing to stop" report kept reading the exclusion as covering that too — the same defect [#1890](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1890) fixed in `watches`, one surface over. An operator whose slot was polled entirely by another channel ran `unwatch`, saw `No active watcher`, and the foreign poller kept running with nothing on the board explaining why.

  `cmd_unwatch` now threads the same `poller_census()` `watches` already computes through to `_report_nothing_stopped`, and discloses — as a count, never a PID or a row — any poller on this exact slot that belongs to another channel or to no channel at all. Nothing here widens what either function may act on.
