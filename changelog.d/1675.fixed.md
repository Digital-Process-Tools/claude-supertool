- **The reserved-key list and the declared-form registry/help split had drifted from what the forwarder and the readers actually do.** `form` and `hint` -- the two keys `#1245`'s `builtin-ops` entries use to document a spelling of another op (`grep-count` for `grep`, `read-grep` for `read`) -- were missing from both `docs/contributing.md`'s reserved-key list and `_OP_CONFIG_RESERVED_KEYS`, so either key on an `ops`-section entry would have leaked to that op's subprocess as `SUPERTOOL_FORM` / `SUPERTOOL_HINT`. And `registry:NAME` answered `ERROR: no op named` for a declared form exactly as it would for a name nobody ever declared, while `help:NAME` answered in full -- a reader who checked one surface concluded the name was real, a reader who checked the other concluded it was not. `registry:NAME` now names a declared form for what it is and points at its parent's entry; `help:NAME` discloses the same fact at the top of its answer.
