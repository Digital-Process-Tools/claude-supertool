- **The `RESOLVE-ERROR: ` sentinel was two independently-typed string literals with no assertion binding them** ([#2229](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2229)) -- the producer in `validators/common/ci_lint_resolve_root.py` and the consumer in `_supertool.py`. The end-to-end behaviour was already covered (#2177), but nothing asserted the two spellings stay equal: a drift would have gone unnoticed until the sentinel silently stopped being recognised, the skip arm was never taken, and the sentence was substituted into the adapter's `{file}` and handed to the callee's option parser as several argv words. The two literals are now pinned equal by a test that loads both real modules, alongside a second real-subprocess-to-real-skip-dict drive colocated with the pin.
