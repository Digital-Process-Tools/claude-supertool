- **The `RESOLVE-ERROR: ` sentinel was two untested, independently-typed string literals with no assertion binding them** ([#2229](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2229)) -- the producer in `validators/common/ci_lint_resolve_root.py` and the consumer in `_supertool.py`. A spelling drift would have gone unnoticed: the sentinel would no longer be recognised, the skip arm would never be taken, and the sentence would be substituted into the adapter's `{file}` and handed to the callee's option parser as several argv words. The two literals are now pinned equal by a test that loads both real modules, and a new end-to-end test drives the real resolver subprocess through the real `_validator_run_one`, proving the skip arm is actually taken.
