- **`classify`'s `could-not-classify` reason for a nonzero-exit spawn now reads the diagnostic `claude` actually wrote, not a bare `spawn exited N`** ([#2060](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2060)). `claude` puts its own error text on **stdout**, not stderr -- a wrong `--model` name, expired auth, and a crash all used to collapse into the same unreadable `spawn exited 1`, discarding the one thing that tells an operator whether to fix their model configuration or their auth. The reason now prefers stderr and falls back to stdout when stderr is empty.
