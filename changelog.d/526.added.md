- **`devto-engagement` and `bluesky-engagement`, two new `watch` sources for comments, replies and reactions on posts you already made** ([#526](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/526)). `devto_status_since` and `bluesky_status_since` already answered "what happened since I last looked" on demand, because neither platform gives a push channel; these sources are the same fetch taught to run itself, as a population poller (`@me`) rather than a per-article watcher, mirroring `gitlab-mr-feed`/`github-issue-feed`. `comment_received` and `reply_received` fire on new comments/replies; `reaction_received` fires on dev.to only when an article's reaction count crosses a fixed threshold (10, 25, 50, ...) since dev.to exposes reactions as one running integer with no per-reaction feed, while on Bluesky each like/repost arrives as its own identified notification and is reported directly, with no threshold needed. A missing credential or a failed fetch reports as `engagement_unreachable`, edge-triggered like every other source's `*_unreachable`, rather than exiting the poller process the way the one-shot `devto`/`bluesky` presets' own auth and request helpers do.
