- **A triage sweep could never become due on its own** ([#2557](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2557)). `.oss.json` set no `triage_route_threshold`, so the label-coverage triage route in the oss plugin's `workspace_routes.py` never evaluated as `over` -- an absent threshold silently skips the route rather than reporting it `not-due` for a reason, so it rendered exactly like a route that ran and found nothing. `release.triggers.triage_after_release` was also disabled, the other half of the same hole, and the two triggers are independent (`next_action.py` only falls through to the label-coverage route once the post-release trigger reports `not-due`). Observed 2026-09-16: three issues (`#2536`, `#2544`, `#2545`) went unlabelled and invisible to lane dispatch for a full tick apart, with the board itself reporting "a triage gap worth a sweep" while `next_action.py --json` ranked triage `not-due` on evidence `triage_route: {"configured": false}` -- the sweep had to be forced by hand. `.oss.json` now sets `triage_route_threshold: 2` (fires on the third unlabelled issue, a deliberate judgement rather than a measurement) and enables `release.triggers.triage_after_release`. A new guard, `tests/test_triage_route_threshold_configured_2557.py`, pins both keys present and sane so this repo's own config cannot silently drop back into the dark state; the routing logic itself lives in the separate `claude-oss` repo and is outside this guard's reach.
