- **`gl-job`'s declared timeout stopped inheriting the budget its own `:trace` fan-out needed** ([#2146](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2146)). #2145 (#2105) raised `gl-job`'s single shared timeout from ~30s to 210s so a multi-id `:trace` fetch (up to `GL_JOB_TRACE_MAX_IDS`, default 6, at up to 30s each) has enough budget — correct for `:trace`, and a 7x-slower failure-detection window for every other mode (`:raw`, `:grep`, `:fail`/`:errors`, plain view), none of which fan out at all. The fan-out is now its own op, `gl-job-trace`, carrying the 210s-class budget alone; `gl-job` dropped back to 45s, sized for its own single-job metadata+trace(+MR) fetch. Calling the old `gl-job:ID[,ID...]:trace` form is refused outright — pointed at `gl-job-trace` — rather than silently run under a timeout too small for more than one id.
