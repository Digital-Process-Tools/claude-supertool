- **The `slack` watch source's own truncation bound sat above the channel's per-attribute cap, so no message over 2048 characters ever delivered any text at all** ([#2059](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2059)). `MESSAGE_CHARS_MAX` was 4000, `SUPERTOOL_CHANNEL_ATTR_MAX` defaults to 2048, and the channel deletes an over-cap attribute whole rather than truncating it — below 2048 the source bound never fired, above it the channel discarded the whole attribute, truncation note included. The poller now derives its bound from the channel's own cap (reading `SUPERTOOL_CHANNEL_ATTR_MAX` itself, the way the channel does, rather than a bare literal picked without reference to it), with headroom for the truncation note it appends.
