- **The ghost-op guard now sweeps every shipped preset manifest, not just the two project config files** ([#2080](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2080)). `tests/test_registry_names_dispatch_1285.py::test_no_config_calls_a_non_builtin_a_builtin` parametrized over `.supertool.json` and `.supertool.example.json` since #1285, but #2025 made `builtin-ops` reachable from a preset manifest too -- `presets/lsp.json`, `presets/vim.json`, and any future one -- and the parametrize list was never widened to match. A control fixture, `presets/ghost.json` naming the retired `blame` op, passed the whole suite outright before this fix. `_BUILTIN_OP_CONFIGS` now globs `presets/*.json` at collection time, so a preset added later is swept without editing the test.
