- **`classify`'s model spawn now actually isolates hooks, CLAUDE.md discovery, plugins and auto-memory, not just tools/MCP/skills** ([#2053](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2053)). The docstring said the classifier was "never told anything about the surrounding session"; probed against the real binary from an ordinary repository, its `SessionStart` hook output, its `CLAUDE.md` and even its own `git status` all reached the model's context. The spawn now runs under `--safe-mode` (not `--bare`, which excludes OAuth auth and broke every non-API-key install) from a fresh, non-git scratch directory per call, so the classifier's context no longer depends on where or under what configuration `classify` was invoked. `tests/test_classify_live_2046.py::test_hooks_claude_md_and_auto_memory_are_actually_isolated` proves it against the real binary, with a positive control confirming the fixture actually leaks under the old flag set.
