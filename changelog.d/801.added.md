- **`gh-pr`'s pending count carries an age, so a progressing run and a wedged one no longer render byte-identically** ([#801](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/801)). `checks: 18 total: 14 passed, 0 failed, 4 pending`, printed twice seven minutes apart, was the same line whether four macOS legs were queued behind runner availability and merging in five minutes, or the run had wedged an hour earlier and nothing further was arriving. Eight PRs sat on that line for the better part of an hour on 2026-08-07 and each one was resolved by hand, in the web UI, for a timestamp. `gh-pr:N` and `gh-pr:N:status` now close the line with `— oldest pending 41m`.

  **The age is the oldest still-pending leg, and the other two candidates were rejected for stated reasons.** The *run's* age is diluted by every leg that already finished. *Time since the last leg changed state* is the tempting one and is wrong in exactly the case this issue was filed from: sixteen legs finish, two stay queued behind a busy pool, and the last observable state change is as old as the sixteenth completion — so a normal queue renders identically to a dead run, and renders as dead. Trading an ambiguous line for a confidently misleading one is not a fix. The oldest pending leg is scoped to the thing the reader is waiting for and nothing else.

  **It is not a staleness alarm.** Nothing prints `STALLED`. A runner with nothing to do is indistinguishable from a runner that cannot work and this repository went 0-for-12 on that inference ([#750](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/750)). The clock is reported; the conclusion is the reader's.

  **A pending leg with no readable start time is disclosed, never dropped.** `gh` spells an unset timestamp `0001-01-01T00:00:00Z` rather than null — it parses cleanly and yields an age of about two thousand years — so it is refused alongside an absent or unparseable one, and the count of refused legs is printed on a disclosure line beneath the tally, saying that the age above is a *floor* rather than the answer. When no pending leg is readable at all the line ends `— oldest pending age UNKNOWN` and the reason is stated underneath. Dropping such legs from the maximum would make the reported age *younger*, which is the reassuring direction and therefore the dangerous one.

  **No new request.** `startedAt` was already in the payload `gh pr view --json statusCheckRollup` returns. `_checks.summarize` appends the note after the whole line, deliberately outside the comma-separated term list: spliced into the pending term it put prose where `tests/test_check_tally_454.py` parses counts back out, which lost the `pending` term entirely and would have let a `2 of 4` be read as a term worth 2. That test caught it, the decoration moved rather than the parser, and the promise that every term sums to `N total` is untouched. The boards that render one tally per row (`gh-prs`, `dashboard`) stay one line wide — the age is opt-in via `summarize_github(..., with_age=True)`.
