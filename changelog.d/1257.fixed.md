- **`gh-pr-merge` now says how far the base has moved since the checks ran** ([#1257](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1257)). The preview opens with a `## Base` block: `BEHIND` by N with the base branch's head sha and date, a stated level, or `UNKNOWN` naming why the compare call did not answer. Three states, because an absent warning and an unasked question rendered identically.

  On 2026-08-10 `master` failed 13 of 15 legs from two PRs that were each 22/22 green, merged four minutes apart — one adding a preset op with no `safety` key, the other adding the whole-tree test requiring one. Disjoint files, so nothing conflict-shaped fired, and the tally was correct: it is a statement about the PR's merge-base, and nothing asked whether that was still the default branch head.

  It discloses and never blocks. A `strict` arm was considered and declined: an opt-in refusal is only typed by somebody who has already decided to be careful, so it adds nothing over reading the line. The distance is asked about `headRefOid`, never `headRefName` — the tally belongs to a commit.
