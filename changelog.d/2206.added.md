- **CI now reports the suite's own time shape instead of leaving `--durations=25` unread** ([#2206](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2206)). A new `Suite duration report` step (`.github/scripts/duration_report.py`) prints the top durations and the slowest test's share of the run's real wall-clock every run, against a committed baseline (`.github/duration-baseline.json`) updated by hand. It is a report, not a gate — nothing here can fail a leg on wall-clock time — and it distinguishes `measured`, `no-baseline` and `could-not-measure` rather than ever printing an empty result that would read as "no hot test".
