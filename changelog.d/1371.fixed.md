- **Seven ops the dispatcher accepts were absent from `docs/operations/index.md`** ([#1371](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1371)). `registry` shipped in [#1363](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1363) and reached neither of the page's two listings; the issue was filed as that one op, and enumerating from `_valid_op_names()` rather than from the report found `check`, `diag`, `guard`, `hover`, `ops-compact` and `rename` in the same state. The opening line also said "~40 ops across five categories" against 44.

  Both listings are now held to the product by `tests/test_ops_index_complete_1371.py`: every dispatchable name must appear in the **Categories** table and in the **full op table**, and the stated count must be the real one. The two are separate assertions because they fail for different readers — the full table is what someone who already knows the name greps, and the Categories table is the only route for someone who does not. `workspace` and `resolve` were listed in the full table and in no category, so they were findable by grep and not by reading.

  `diag`, `hover` and `rename` get a new **LSP-backed** category pointing at `docs/mcp-integration.md`, which documented them the whole time.

  **The page also named three ops that do not exist**, found by reviewing the fix rather than by the issue. `blame` had a row with a syntax cell, and `blame:PATH:LINE` answers `unknown operation` — the op is `git-blame`, from the `git` preset. `read-grep` and `grep-count` sat in the Categories table beside real names; they are argument forms of `read` and `grep`, not invocations. Completeness and honesty are separate properties and the first test only had the first, so a second assertion now refuses any name on the page that the dispatcher would not accept, checked against builtins **plus** the loaded preset ops so a legitimate `git-blame` row passes. The `resolve` row also documented only `resolve:SYMBOL`, omitting the `:FILE` argument that a Python relative import needs and without which it is reported `external`.
