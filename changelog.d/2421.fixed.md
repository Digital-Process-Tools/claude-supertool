- **The `supertool-no-cut` jit rule now tells a blocked caller the refusal is recoverable** ([#2421](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2421)). Three dispatched agents wedged for two to four hours in one night after piping a supertool op through `head`: each was refused by this `mode: block` rule and never sent another tool call, `running` per `ListAgents` the whole time rather than dead. The rule body explained the policy at length without ever telling the caller, in an imperative sentence near the top, that the refusal is not terminal and what to do about it right now. `.claude/jit-context/tools/00-manual/supertool-no-cut.md` now opens with exactly that instruction, ahead of the policy explanation, still inside the 3,200-byte per-match budget `tests/test_jit_rule_body_budget_1433.py` enforces. Whether `agent-unreachable`'s own diagnosis bar should also learn to tell a wedged agent from a dead one is a design question in `claude-oss`'s `dispatch.md`, out of this repository's blast radius.
