- **The coverage gate no longer exempts itself from the coverage gate** ([#991](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/991)). `.github/scripts/` was measured on every run, printed in the report, and floored by nothing — it could go to zero without reddening a build. It now carries two floors: 92% on `.github/scripts/coverage_gate.py` by name, and 92% on the rest of the directory.

  Two entries and not one because the measurement is what decided it. The bucket read 82.65%, made of `assemble_changelog.py` at 93.95%, `junit_summary.py` at 94.12%, and **the gate itself at 46.43%** — the least covered thing the gate measured was the gate. A single floor on the bucket average is the arrangement in which the two tested files pay for the untested one, which is the argument `coverage_gate.py` already makes for giving `supertool.py` and `presets/` separate floors, applied one level down.

  What the 46% was hiding matters more than the number: `report()` had no test at all. The function that decides pass from fail, the branch that turns "nothing was measured" into a failure rather than a 0% that clears a floor of 0, and the stale-floor advisory were all uncovered, along with `measure()` and `main()`'s reporting path. `report()` could have been changed to `return 0` unconditionally and all twelve pytest legs would have stayed green. The refusal branches added in #877 were, by contrast, the covered part.

  `tests/test_coverage_gate_floor_991.py` covers the verdict function — a below-floor bucket returns 1, each floor fires on its own bucket with the others at 100%, an unmeasured bucket fails rather than passing, the advisory fires past `_SLACK` and stays quiet inside it — and the floors above were measured after those tests existed rather than pinning the accident of the day.

  Prefix matching is longest-match now rather than first-match, in `classify()` and `_bucket_key()` alike. With a file entry and the directory containing it both declared, first-match made the verdict depend on dict insertion order: reordering the literal for readability would have moved the gate onto the directory floor and printed a number that looked just as convincing.
