- **`git-commit:::amend` made a commit whose subject was the word `amend`** ([#962](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/962)). There is no amend route in this op, so a word that is unambiguously an *instruction* in git's vocabulary was silently reinterpreted as *content* — and the receipt printed a green tick over it, with nothing distinguishing "you asked to amend and I did" from "you asked to amend and I named a commit after your request". The recovery is `git reset --soft HEAD~1`, which is the git operation people most reliably get wrong under time pressure. `amend` and `--amend`, as the whole message, are now refused before anything is staged: nothing is added, nothing is committed, and the refusal names the gap it is standing in for.

  **It refuses under [#692](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/692)'s contract, after the `Repo:` / `HEAD before:` stamp rather than before it.** Every other refusal path in this op names the repository and branch it refused you in, and this is the one that hands the reader a raw `git commit --amend` to run — so which checkout and which branch is the first thing they need, and the one thing the op knows and they may not. Nothing is staged either way, so the earlier position bought nothing.

  **The refusal is an exact match on the whole message, not a heuristic.** "This message looks like a git subcommand" drifts — the first thing it takes down is `revert the revert`, and every later widening of it is invisible until it refuses somebody's real subject. Exactly `amend` or `--amend`, stripped and lowercased, with nothing else in the message, cannot drift.

  **And it is not a dead end, because `amend` is a legal English word.** The refusal names `SUPERTOOL_ALLOW_LITERAL_AMEND=1` and prints the exact call to re-run with it, using the same off-vocabulary as the co-author trailer beside it. It also names the two raw-git forms (`git commit --amend --no-edit` / `-m 'NEW SUBJECT'`) and says plainly not to amend a commit that is already pushed — *and that this op has no way to tell you whether it was*, which is the reason an amend route was deliberately **not** added here. That route needs its own refusal for published history and is a feature, not something to grow out of the side of a bugfix.
