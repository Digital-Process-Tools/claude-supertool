- **`presets/slack.json`'s `syntax` field no longer advertises the bare-path arm `#2039` withdrew** ([#2075](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2075)). `#2039` removed bare-path auto-detect from `slack_publish` and updated three of four surfaces (`docs/presets/slack.md`, its own changelog fragment) -- the manifest's own `syntax` string, the one `help:slack_publish` renders, was the outlier and still read `TEXT_OR_FILE_OR_file://PATH`. A caller writing `slack_publish:C0123|notes.md` on the strength of that signature silently posted the literal string `notes.md`. `syntax` is now `TEXT_OR_file://PATH`, matching the doc and the code, and `tests/test_slack_manifest_syntax_2075.py` pins the manifest against both the doc's wording and the op's actual behavior so the two cannot drift apart again unnoticed.
