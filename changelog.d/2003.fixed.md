- **`jit-index`'s tools-row shape check now refuses anything other than 6 or 7 fields, matching its own refusal message** ([#2003](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/2003)). The predicate at `validators/jit-index/jit-index.py:153` was `len(fields) >= 4`, so a hand-typed row missing a column (4 or 5 fields) or one with a stray extra tab (9 fields) was silently accepted as a well-formed tools row, while the refusal two branches down already said a tools row has "6 or 7" fields (claude-jit-context 0.6.0's 7th `requires` column, #1992) -- #1992 corrected the sentence, not the branch it describes. A row that slips through this way is exactly the shape `CLAUDE.md` warns about: the hook then reads `mode` out of the wrong column and a `block` rule silently stops blocking, indistinguishable from a rule that simply never matches. `tests/test_jit_index_row_shape_2003.py` pins the exercise table from the issue -- 6 and 7 fields accepted, 4/5/9 refused as a shape error that actually flips `ok` to `False`.
