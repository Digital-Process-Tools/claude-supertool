- **`write:@-` got 44 op names in alphabetical order instead of being pointed at `paste`** ([#1303](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1303)). The harness tool is called `Write`, and `.claude/jit-context/tools/00-manual/harness-tools-blocked.md` — which fires on every `Write` attempt in this repo — maps it to `paste`. The tool taught the mapping in one place and forgot it in the other. `write` and `vi` are now the two entries of a synonym table read by the unknown-op message, and additions to it need the same kind of receipt: everything inferable is inferred by the near-miss rules from [#1222](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1222) instead, where the rule can be argued with.
- **The developer agent's own brief prescribed a `git-commit` form that refuses by construction** ([#1303](https://github.com/Digital-Process-Tools/claude-supertool/issues/1303)). `.claude/agents/opensource-developer.md` spelled the commit step `git-commit:::MESSAGE` with no paths. `git-commit` never stages, so that form is correctly refused — meaning the first commit attempt of every delegated run failed on advice the repo itself handed the agent. The line now names `:::PATHS`, `:::--all` and the `@-` payload route, and `tests/test_developer_brief_commit_form_1303.py` extracts every colon form the brief prescribes and *runs* it against a scratch repo, so a prose rewrite cannot quietly restore a form that does not work. The issue also reports the same line in `CLAUDE.md`; it is not there — `git-commit` appears nowhere in that file.
